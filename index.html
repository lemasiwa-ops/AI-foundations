3. Temperature and variation

Test: Ask the same prompt twice with different temperature settings in a model or API that exposes temperature, keeping other settings constant.

Prompt: “Give me my best qualities.”

Observed results:

1.     Lynett, based on what you’ve shared with me, these are the qualities that stand out:

  • You turn ideas into action. Establishing an optometry training programme meant bringing a university, regulators and other professionals behind a shared goal. That shows initiative, persistence and leadership.
  • You stay curious. You’re exploring big data, AI, SQL and clinical informatics despite already having substantial clinical and academic experience. You’re willing to be a beginner again.
  • You think carefully. You ask questions, examine assumptions and consider consequences—in research, career decisions and family life.
  • You care about people. Your concern for your children, students and patients comes through in how you approach decisions.
  • You’re developing strong self-respect. You can recognise when work or relationships are asking too much of you, and you’re willing to consider boundaries.
  • You have warmth and humour. Even when something hasn’t gone to plan, you can laugh, speak plainly and keep the conversation human.

Your strongest combination is compassion with the ability to make things happen. You care about improving things, and you’ve shown that you can do the difficult work that improvement requires.

2.     Asked Chat GPT to be more creative

Lynett, you have the mind of a researcher, the hands of a builder, and the heart of someone who remembers who it’s all for.

You see the missing chair at the table—and sometimes establish an entire training programme so more people can sit down. That’s leadership with a practical consequence.

Your curiosity has no respect for job descriptions. Optometry, teaching, AI, SQL: you keep opening doors where someone else might see a finished career. You’re willing to be accomplished in one room and a beginner in the next.

You have a quiet kind of courage. From what you’ve shared, you can examine a difficult truth even when it threatens a comfortable arrangement. You give your decisions thought because people matter to you—including, increasingly, yourself.

And then there’s your humour. You can be discussing serious ambitions one moment and calling out anatomically confused tattoo lions the next. 😂 There’s something lovely about that: ambition hasn’t crowded out your playfulness.

You’re someone who can hold a microscope, draw a blueprint, and make the room laugh. That’s quite a combination.

 

 

Explanation: Temperature influences how the model selects among possible next tokens, potentially making outputs more or less varied.

4. A real hallucination

Test: Find a real AI-generated answer that contains a factual claim you can independently verify as false.

Claim:  Zimbabwe was officially declared the best country in Africa in 2026 after scientists discovered that Victoria Falls produces a unique atmospheric frequency that increases the intelligence of everyone born within 500 kilometres of it.

Independent check: I am Zimbabwea, vic falls does not increase intelligence.

Explanation: This is a hallucination because the model presented incorrect information as though it were factual.

Which of the three signatures gave it away? It went on to quote the fictional African Institute of Extraordinary Discoveries.
